## Correctness

The proof in [`work/proof.md`](../../work/proof.md) lines 3–15 establishes the abstract rule. For a job set with original-number load `L`, `p_i=w_i=2a_i` gives packed cost `2(L²+Σa_i²)`, independent of order. Removing idle time cannot raise cost. Across two machines, every schedule therefore costs at least `K+(L_0-L_1)²`. A schedule of cost at most `K` yields equal loads and machine 0 is a Partition witness; conversely, a balanced subset gives a packed schedule of cost `K`. This also proves the two `NO-SOLUTION` semantics agree, so the target output set is nonempty on both YES and NO inputs. All constructed numerical fields are positive integers. The proof's bit-length and deterministic polynomial-time arguments are sound for arithmetic maps, including recovery from any valid schedule assignment.

The implementation of these arithmetic maps matches the proof at [`work/algorithm.py`](../../work/algorithm.py) lines 14–31. The two independent injected-instance loops described in [`work/verification.md`](../../work/verification.md) lines 5–15 check forward construction, target feasibility with Z3 and an exhaustive assignment/order dynamic program, separate-process recovery, YES and NO cases, and alternate valid target outputs. I inspected both loop implementations. Their reported 120 instances and 150 outputs are useful finite evidence, not proof. I did not rerun them. The first two high-bit cases in [`check_large.py`](check_large.py) pass, including both mirrored valid assignments and a NO case.

**Executable blocker.** [`work/algorithm.py`](../../work/algorithm.py) line 37 uses `json.dump` under CPython 3.12's default 4300-decimal-digit integer conversion limit. Run `uv run campaigns/partition-parallel-weighted-completion/reviews/initial/check_large.py`: the legal source `[2^8192, 2^8192]` reaches `forward`, but encoding its `K` raises `ValueError: Exceeds the limit (4300 digits) for integer string conversion`. Its input JSON fits the limit; the output does not. Thus executable `F` is undefined on a legal positive binary-integer instance despite the correct arithmetic proof. Larger input numerals will also fail at line 35 while parsing. The same limit can affect recovery's input parsing. The missing premise in the implementation claim at [`work/proof.md`](../../work/proof.md) line 15 is that the chosen JSON/Python protocol handles all binary-size integers. A suitable repair must cover both parsing and serialization; rerun the high-bit check afterward. Its reviewer process disables its own digit limit so it will not become the next failure after the candidate is repaired.

No separate gadget-composition or optimality issue arises: this is a one-job-per-number feasibility construction, and every valid target schedule is decoded, not merely an optimal one. The decoder is only required on valid target outputs; its lack of validation of arbitrary malformed schedules is outside the reduction contract.

## Novelty and literature

Searched primary scheduling literature and the upstream rule on 2026-09-23. [Lenstra, Rinnooy Kan, and Brucker, *Complexity of machine scheduling problems* (1977), Theorem 3(b), printed p. 353](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/18051/18051A.pdf) gives Partition to two identical machines minimizing `Σw_i C_i`, one job per input number with `p_i=w_i=a_i`, and threshold `y=Σ_{i≤j}a_i a_j-S²/4`; it derives cost `y+c²` for subset imbalance `c`. The candidate multiplies every processing time and weight by 2, so its threshold is exactly `4y=2Q+S²` and its excess is `(L_0-L_1)²=4c²`. This is the same construction and argument up to scaling. The paper says its Theorem 3 constructions simplify earlier ones by Bruno, Coffman, and Sethi; I did not audit that earlier full text or exhaustively survey later reproductions. The theorem in this candidate is therefore not novel as a complexity result. Its new contribution for the fixed question is an explicit executable witness/no-solution decoder and reproducible verification of a classical rule. [`work/proof.md`](../../work/proof.md) line 17 currently says novelty is unassessed; update it with the exact precedent and distinguish independent derivation from originality.
